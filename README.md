# Party-Games
General instruction: Pass around a phone with the website open. Each player taps on their player number to reveal their role, and then taps again to hide it before sending the phone to the next person.

How to play each game:

Charades   
Divide the group into two teams. Get a 1-2 minute timer ready. Each group takes turns. One person from the first group gets a word and the rest of their group has to guess the word just from what the person acts out. No sounds allowed. Repeat with the second group and do as many rounds as you want, keeping track of the score.

Spy  
Everyone gets a location, except the spy. The spy gets a card that says: "You are the spy!". The people that know the location have to guess who the spy is, and the spy has to guess what the location is. It is probably a good idea to have a timer and vote people out after certain intervalls. The spy wins if they shout out the right location, and everyone else wins if they vote out the spy. 

Imposter artist  
Everyone gets something to draw, except the imposter. The imposter gets a card that says: "You are the imposter!". A paper is sent around and each player has a different colour pen. Each player can draw one (subtle) line at a time to create the drawing. Do as many rounds as necessary to reveal the imposter or for the imposter to guess what everyone is drawing. 

Mr White  
Everyone gets the same word, except one person that gets a slightly different word: they are Mr White! Mr White wins if they guess the word everyone else has, and the collective wins if they guess who Mr White is. Mr White doesn't know they are Mr White so this one is a bit tricky!

Question Spy  
This one is a bit silly. It is the same as Mr White except with questions this time! One person gets the opposite question as everyone else so you have to be extra careful with how you answer and defend your answers.

Psychologist  
Here you can either randomly allocate a psychologist by passing around the phone and letting the website decide; or you can choose one and then everyone else looks at the other cards without letting the psychologist see. 
The psychologist has to figure out what collective behaviour everyone in the group is displaying. For example, while the psychologist asks questions, the other players all have to pretend they have mosquitos around them trying to sting them. 
You can also choose your own rules, but make sure the psychologist doesn't hear!
